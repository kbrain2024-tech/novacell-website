NovaCell Therapy FAQ
Clear Answers to Common Questions

Find concise, scientifically validated explanations regarding cellular voltage, device safety, protocol applications, and clinical mechanisms.

Q1. How does NovaCell Therapy differ from conventional TENS units?

Unlike TENS units that use high milliampere currents merely to block pain nerve signals temporarily, NovaCell delivers microcurrents matched to biological cellular levels (microamperes). It directly charges cellular membrane voltage (-50mV) and aligns biological polarity, promoting true cellular repair rather than temporary symptom masking.

Q2. Is bio-electric microcurrent therapy safe?

Yes. NovaCell operates within physiological microcurrent thresholds that mirror the human body's natural bio-electric signals. It is non-invasive, gentle, and designed with real-time biofeedback resistance adaptation.

Q3. Can individuals use NovaCell at home, or is it only for clinics?

NovaCell provides tailored programs for both hospital clinics and personal home care. Comprehensive video guides and clinical instruction ensure home caregivers can practice protocols safely and effectively.
