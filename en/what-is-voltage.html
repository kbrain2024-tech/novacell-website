Biophysical Principle

Cellular Voltage: The Master Key to Regeneration

Healthy human cells operate at approximately -20mV to -25mV during baseline homeostasis. However, to repair damaged tissue and generate new healthy cells, biological systems demand a boost up to -50mV.

When chronic illness, physical trauma, or sustained stress depletes cellular voltage below -20mV, cells lose the electrical energy necessary to eliminate metabolic wastes, maintain membrane integrity, and communicate with the brain. Chronic pain, inflammation, and fatigue inevitably follow.

"Chronic disease occurs when cellular voltage falls below the threshold needed for repair. Healing is the restoration of voltage."
